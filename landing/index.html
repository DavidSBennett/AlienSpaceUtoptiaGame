The Historians

A card game about how history gets written — and how scholars argue about it.

You're a historian building a career. Draw primary-source evidence, marshal it behind a conclusion, and publish articles and books that actually hold together — attending conferences and peer-reviewing your rivals as you rise from graduate student to endowed chair.
