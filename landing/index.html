The Historians

A card game about how history gets written — and how scholars argue about it.

Designed by Dr. David S. Bennett

Congratulations, Doctor — welcome to the guild. You’ve just started a one-year appointment at a prestigious university. Your office is beautiful: large wooden desk, centuries-old oaks, ivy climbing the buildings out the window.

But you need a permanent position. You are hungry for more: the Pulitzer, a name that outlives you. Will you hire a literary agent, court a publicist, or pour everything into research funding and archive visits? It all serves the same goals. Writing the story of the past. Publishing the next great argument.
