The Historians

A card game about how history gets written — and how scholars argue about it.

Congratulations, Doctor — welcome to the Historians guild. As of right now, you hold a visiting professor position at a prestigious institution, but you are hungry for more: the Pulitzer, the Francis Perkins Award, or simply the immortality that comes from fundamentally changing the direction of the discipline.

Every path is open. Will you hire a literary agent, court a publicist, or pour everything into research funding and archive visits? However you build your study, it all serves one goal — publishing the next great argument.
