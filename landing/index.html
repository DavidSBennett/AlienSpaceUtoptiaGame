The Historians

A card game about how history gets written — and how scholars argue about it.

You're a historian building a career. Draw primary-source evidence from the archive, marshal it behind a conclusion, and publish articles and books that actually hold together.

Attend conferences, peer-review your rivals' manuscripts, and rise from graduate student to endowed chair — learning how historians make, test, and defend a claim.
